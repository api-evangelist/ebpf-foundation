<p><em>This work is supported by the eBPF Foundation&#8217;s <a href="https://ebpf.foundation/funding-opportunities/community-advocacy-fellowship-program/">Community &amp; Advocacy Fellowship Program</a>, written by eBPF Community &amp; Advocacy Fellow Teodor Janez Podobnik.</em></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In October 2025, I was honored to receive the </span><b>Inaugural Community &amp; Advocacy Fellowship </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">from the eBPF Foundation in recognition of my ongoing contributions to the eBPF community. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As part of this work, I hosted three podcast conversations focused on different aspects of eBPF: </span></p>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://ebpfchirp.substack.com/p/interview-with-avi-lumelsky-ai-security" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>Avi Lumelsky</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">,</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">AI Security Researcher at Oligo Security</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, discussed the intersection of eBPF and AI, including application-aware runtime security and approaches to correlating published CVEs with runtime detection signals. </span></li>
<li><a href="https://ebpfchirp.substack.com/p/interview-with-rafael-david-tinoco" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>Rafael David Tinoco</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">,</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">Senior Software Engineer at Garnet</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, covered CI/CD runtime security using eBPF, the trade-offs of perf events and ring buffer mechanisms, and considerations in designing eBPF-based stacks. </span></li>
<li><a href="https://ebpfchirp.substack.com/p/interview-with-henrik-rexed-cncf" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>Henrik Rexed</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">,</span> <span style="font-weight: 400;">CNCF Ambassador and Cloud Native Advocate at Dynatrace</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, reflected on where eBPF is an appropriate (and inappropriate) choice, and discussed the practical question of whether runtime security systems should block or alert on detected behavior.</span></li>
</ul>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In addition, I developed and presented my personal eBPF project, </span><a href="https://ebpfchirp.substack.com/p/runtime-security-in-github-actions" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><b>Kayrndor: GitHub</b> <b>Action Runtime Security Agent</b></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">,</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> at the Backend Meetup in Slovenia. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The fellowship’s financial support also made it possible for me to organize the </span><b>first-ever eBPF Meetup in Slovenia</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, where engineers from across the industry gathered to explore both beginner and advanced eBPF topics, exchange experiences, and strengthen the local community. </span></p>
