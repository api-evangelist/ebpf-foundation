May 2026 marked the end of my inaugural Community & Advocacy Fellowship with the eBPF Foundation, awarded in recognition of my ongoing contributions to the eBPF community. During the first half of the fellowship , I focused on expanding my community efforts across multiple formats, including eBPF podcasts, local meetups, conference talks, and hands-on coding labs. In the second half, I focused on the initiatives which I identified as generating the most impact: interactive eBPF Coding Labs, eBPF 1-on-1 mentorship and technical talks that help developers and platform engineers learn eBPF.
