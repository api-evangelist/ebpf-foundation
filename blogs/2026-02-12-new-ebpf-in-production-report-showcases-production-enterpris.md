<h2><b>TL;DR</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">New research from the eBPF Foundation highlights how enterprises are achieving measurable cost savings, performance gains, risk reduction, and operational efficiencies with eBPF in production. The </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">eBPF In Production: An Overview of Compelling Enterprise Outcomes Using eBPF</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> report showcases real-world deployments from organizations including Cloudflare, Netflix, ByteDance, and Rakuten, and demonstrates how eBPF has become a foundational technology for modern infrastructure. The report is available now as a free download.</span></p>
<p><b>FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE</b></p>
<p><em><span style="font-weight: 400;">Latest research from the eBPF Foundation demonstrates how organizations are achieving measurable ROI with eBPF at global scale</span></em></p>
<p><b>SAN FRANCISCO – February 12, 2026</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> – The eBPF Foundation, which advances eBPF’s upstream development and community and promotes its secure adoption across platforms, today announced the availability of its newest research report, </span><a href="https://www.linuxfoundation.org/hubfs/eBPF/eBPF%20In%20Production%20Report.pdf"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">eBPF In Production</span></i></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Designed for executive and senior technical leaders, the report focuses on tangible business outcomes and their associated ROI. It consolidates real-world examples of organizations deploying eBPF in production and documents measurable improvements in performance, infrastructure efficiency, security posture, and operational scale.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As the report, authored by tech journalist Bill Doerrfeld, explains eBPF has evolved into a mature, production-grade infrastructure technology with clear benefits over existing solutions. Embedded within products, projects, and tools spanning networking, observability, runtime security, and application governance, eBPF enables organizations to implement advanced functionality in the Linux kernel without disruptive kernel rewrites, risks of crashing the system, or excessive overhead.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“eBPF has moved decisively from experimentation to enterprise standard,” said Bill Mulligan, eBPF Governing Board Member from Isovalent. “This report demonstrates that leading organizations are not just piloting eBPF, they are standardizing on it to reduce costs, mitigate risk, and improve system performance at massive scale.”</span></p>
<h3><b>Key Insights From the Report</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Drawing on documented case studies and public production benchmarks, the report highlights consistent patterns of success across industries:</span></p>
<h3><b>Reduced Infrastructure Costs</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Organizations are leveraging deep kernel-level visibility to optimize CPU, GPU, and network utilization:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Datadog reduced CPU usage by 35% using an eBPF-based connection tracker.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meta reduced CPU cycles by up to 20% using its eBPF-driven Strobelight profiler.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Polar Signals cut cross-zone traffic costs by 50% with eBPF-based observability.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>Increased Operational Efficiency and Scale</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">eBPF’s low-overhead execution model enables leaner, more scalable infrastructure:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">LinkedIn reduced Kafka log volume by 70% using an eBPF observability agent.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Seznam.cz doubled throughput while reducing CPU usage by 72x with eBPF load balancing.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">DoorDash achieved 40% less memory usage, 98% fewer restarts, and 80% faster deployments after migrating to eBPF-based monitoring.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>Risk Mitigation and Attack Prevention</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Kernel-level enforcement and telemetry enable faster, more precise security controls:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">SentinelOne detects and stops ransomware attempts in under one second using eBPF-based architecture.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Cloudflare mitigated DDoS attacks peaking above 7 Tbps using eBPF-based XDP programs.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">CoreTech mitigated a 1 Tbps DDoS attack without downtime using eBPF-powered scrubbing.</span></li>
</ul>
<h3><b>Improved Developer Velocity</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">By centralizing observability and policy enforcement at the kernel layer, organizations reduce instrumentation burden, troubleshooting complexity, and compliance friction, enabling faster innovation across platform and security teams.</span></p>
<h3><b>Featured Enterprise Case Studies</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The report takes a deep dive into four global organizations using eBPF as a strategic infrastructure layer:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><b>Cloudflare</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, where eBPF underpins networking, observability, and terabit-scale DDoS mitigation.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><b>Netflix</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, which uses eBPF for network defense, “noisy neighbor” detection, and large-scale telemetry collection supporting more than 325 million subscribers.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><b>ByteDance</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, which improved throughput by 10% across infrastructure supporting approximately one million servers by adopting eBPF-based networking.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><b>Rakuten Mobile</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, where eBPF powers anomaly detection, security enforcement, and observability within a cloud-native telecom environment.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Together, these case studies demonstrate that eBPF is no longer an isolated tool for networking or observability, it is becoming a modular foundation that unifies performance, security, and telemetry at the kernel level.</span></p>
<h3><b>A Strategic Infrastructure Foundation</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The report concludes that eBPF is now woven into the fabric of modern Linux-based systems and should be viewed as a strategic infrastructure layer rather than a niche technology. Organizations that treat eBPF as a unified foundation across networking, observability, and security are realizing compound benefits in efficiency, visibility, and risk reduction.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As steward of the eBPF ecosystem, the eBPF Foundation plays a central role in advancing upstream innovation, supporting collaboration across vendors and end users, and accelerating the adoption of secure, programmable infrastructure.</span></p>
