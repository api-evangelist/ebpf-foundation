Researchers at Virginia Tech and the University of British Columbia will develop open source approaches to improve eBPF diagnostics and secure virtual machine observability Summary The eBPF Foundation has awarded $100,000 in grants to researchers at Virginia Tech and the University of British Columbia. Two $50,000 grants will support research into eBPF developer diagnostics and secure virtual machine observability, addressing challenges in usability and security. Grant recipients will open source project deliverables, making the resulting research available to the broader eBPF community.
