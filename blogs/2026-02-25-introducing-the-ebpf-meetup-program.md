<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The eBPF ecosystem is growing, and so is the demand for strong local communities where developers can meet, share ideas, and create the next generations of eBPF projects, products, and companies.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The eBPF Foundation is excited to launch the eBPF Meetup Program, a new initiative designed to support and grow local eBPF communities around the world. This program empowers community members to organize local events focused on eBPF use cases and real-world production experience.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As eBPF continues to grow, knowledge sharing is becoming just as important as code, and local meetups play a critical role in that evolution.</span></p>
<h2><b>How the Program Supports Organizers</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The eBPF Meetup Program provides financial support to approved eBPF-focused events to help reduce the burden of organizing local gatherings. This support can be used for typical meetup related expenses such as food, venue costs, swag, and other essentials.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Events must be hosted on community.cncf.io or meetup.com and primarily focused on eBPF technology or use cases.</span></p>
<p><a href="https://ebpf.foundation/meetup-program/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">See the full reimbursement guidelines</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></a></p>
<h2><b>Building the next phase of the ecosystem</b></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As the Foundation continues to invest in research, upstream development, and programs like </span><a href="https://ebpf.foundation/funding-opportunities/research-fund/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">grants</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and </span><a href="https://ebpf.foundation/funding-opportunities/community-advocacy-fellowship-program/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">fellowships</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, the Meetup Program represents another step toward strengthening the global eBPF community.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you&#8217;re using, developing, or just interested in eBPF, consider hosting a meetup and sharing your journey. If you&#8217;re new to the space, join a local event and start learning. If you want to meet other eBPF enthusiasts in your area, there are already meetups in </span><a href="https://kernel-ebpf-meetup.github.io/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">San Francisco</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, </span><a href="https://ebpf.connpass.com/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tokyo</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and </span><a href="https://www.meetup.com/de-de/ebpf-vienna/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vienna</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and you can always start your own!</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">We look forward to seeing more eBPF communities take shape around the world.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Questions? Reach out to info@ebpf.foundation.</span></p>
